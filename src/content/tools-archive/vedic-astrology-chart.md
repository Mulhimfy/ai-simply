Vedic Astrology Chart generates a sidereal birth chart from your date, time, and place of birth, then uses AI to explain what the chart contains in ordinary language. Alongside the main chart it calculates nakshatras, the D9 (Navamsa) divisional chart, dasha periods, and the other divisional charts that Jyotish practice draws on.

The interpretation layer is the point of difference. Chart calculators are common and largely interchangeable; what tends to stop newcomers is that the output is a dense grid of symbols with no explanation. Generating readable notes against each placement makes the chart legible to someone who has not studied the system.

Treat the readings as interpretation rather than fact — astrology is not a predictive science, and the tool is best understood as a structured way to explore a tradition.
