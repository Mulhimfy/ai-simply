Express Briefs starts from a single brief — what you are posting about and who it is for — and produces captions shaped for each platform you intend to post on. The output differs per channel rather than being one caption pasted everywhere: LinkedIn and Reddit want length and context, TikTok and Instagram want a hook, WhatsApp is closer to a direct message.

The tool is aimed at operators rather than agencies, with templates for real estate, automotive, e-commerce, restaurants, fitness, recruitment, events, local services, hospitality, fashion resale, and wedding vendors. That framing suits the business owner posting their own listings, who needs the same announcement in eight places and does not want to rewrite it eight times.

## Key Features

- One brief, captions for eight platforms
- Per-platform tone and length rather than one shared caption
- Templates for eleven industry verticals
- Covers WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, and Quora

## Best For

Small business owners and solo marketers announcing the same thing across several channels, particularly in listings-driven trades like property, cars, and events.
