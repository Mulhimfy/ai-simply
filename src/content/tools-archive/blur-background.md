Blur Background handles two related jobs on the same image: blurring a background to push focus onto the subject, and removing it outright for a clean cutout. It runs in the browser, so there is no desktop editor to install for what is usually a one-off edit.

Privacy is the use case worth calling out. Photos taken at home or in an office routinely capture documents, screens, addresses, and other people in the background, and blurring is a faster fix than cropping or retaking the shot. The same tool covers the more ordinary needs — portrait depth-of-field and clean product images for a listing.

## Key Features

- Background blur with adjustable strength
- Full background removal for cutouts
- Runs in the browser with no install
- Suited to portraits and product photography

## Best For

Anyone who needs to obscure what is behind the subject before sharing a photo, plus sellers producing clean product images without a full editor.
