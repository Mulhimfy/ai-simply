Lovspark is an AI rendering platform for interior designers, architects, and studios. You feed it whatever you already have — a text brief, a phone photo of an empty room, a hand sketch, or a floor plan — and it produces photorealistic renders, staged spaces, and concept variations from it. The work happens on an infinite canvas where each generation is a node, so you can branch a design into several directions, compare them side by side, and leave comments for clients or teammates without exporting anything.

The platform ships as a set of 20+ named "skills" rather than one generic prompt box. Virtual Staging fills a bare room with styled furnishings; Architecture Render turns line drawings into finished visualizations; Product Add drops catalog items into a scene with automatic scaling and lighting; Targeted Material Swap changes one surface without disturbing the rest of the frame. Scene to Moodboard pulls a render back into a presentation board with PANTONE references, and Spatial Storyboard generates multi-angle sequences and video walkthroughs from a single space.

## Key Features

- Photorealistic renders from text prompts, reference images, sketches, or floor plans
- Virtual staging that furnishes empty rooms in a chosen style
- Architecture Render for turning drawings into finished visualizations
- Product Add with automatic scaling and lighting for catalog items
- Targeted Material Swap to edit one material in isolation
- Material extraction and analysis to identify finishes and furnishings in a scene
- Scene to Moodboard with PANTONE color references
- Spatial Storyboard for multi-angle renders and video walkthroughs
- Infinite canvas with multi-node workflows, comments, and collaboration

## Pricing

You can start your first few designs free with no credit card. Beyond that it's a monthly credit subscription: Creator at $19.99/mo for 2,000 credits, Pro at $44.99/mo for 5,000 credits with priority processing and more parallel jobs, and Max at $99.99/mo for 12,000 credits. Launch discounts cut the first month substantially, annual billing is available, and commercial usage rights are included on every plan.

## Best For

Interior designers, architects, and design studios who need client-ready visuals fast — especially anyone currently waiting on a 3D artist to turn a floor plan or mood direction into something a client can actually react to.
