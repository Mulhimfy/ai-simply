Getimg.ai is a browser-based suite that bundles most of the open image-generation ecosystem into one interface. Instead of committing to a single model, you pick from dozens of options — Stable Diffusion 1.5 and SDXL, Flux, and various community checkpoints — and generate from text prompts. Around that core it layers editing tools, so the same platform handles creation, retouching, and expansion of images.

What differentiates Getimg from a single-model tool like Midjourney is breadth and control. It offers an AI canvas for outpainting beyond the original frame, inpainting to replace regions, ControlNet-style guidance for pose and composition, and the ability to train your own custom model on a set of images (useful for a consistent character or product). It also exposes an API, positioning it as both a consumer tool and a developer backend, competing with Leonardo.ai, Playground, and running local Stable Diffusion.

## Key Features

- Text-to-image across SDXL, Flux, and many community models
- Inpainting and outpainting for edits and canvas expansion
- AI canvas for iterative, region-based editing
- ControlNet-style guidance for pose, depth, and composition
- Custom model training on your own uploaded images
- Face fix, upscaling, and background tools
- REST API for developers

## Pricing

- **Free**: A limited number of images per month (roughly 100)
- **Basic (around $12/month)**: A few thousand images monthly
- **Starter/Pro (around $29+/month)**: Higher monthly image quotas and priority
- **API/Enterprise**: Usage-based credits for programmatic access

## Best For

Creators and small teams who want one platform for generating and editing AI images across many models, and developers who need an image-generation API without hosting their own Stable Diffusion infrastructure.

## Limitations

The sheer number of models and settings gives power but can overwhelm newcomers who just want a clean prompt box. The free tier's monthly cap is modest, so regular use pushes you to a subscription, and while output is strong, the most polished aesthetic results still often come from Midjourney. Custom model training also takes some trial and error to get right.
