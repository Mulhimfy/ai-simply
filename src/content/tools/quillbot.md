QuillBot is one of the most widely used AI writing helpers, best known for its paraphraser. Paste in a sentence or paragraph and it rewrites the wording and structure while trying to preserve the meaning — handy for clarifying a clunky draft, varying repetitive phrasing, or adapting formal text into something plainer. It reports tens of millions of users, many of them students and non-native English writers.

QuillBot is owned by Learneo (the parent company behind Course Hero), which acquired it in 2021. Over time the product grew from a single paraphrasing box into a small writing suite: a summarizer, a grammar checker, a citation generator, and a translator. That positions it against Grammarly on grammar and Wordtune on rephrasing, though paraphrasing remains its core draw.

## Key Features

- Multiple paraphrasing modes (Standard, Fluency, Formal, Creative, Shorten, Expand)
- Synonym slider to control how much wording changes
- AI Summarizer that condenses long documents into key points or a paragraph
- Grammar and spelling checker with inline suggestions
- Citation generator for APA, MLA, and Chicago
- Chrome extension and Microsoft Word add-in
- Built-in translator for cross-language rewriting

## Pricing

- **Free**: paraphrasing capped at a limited word count per pass, with a subset of modes
- **Premium (~$10/month billed annually)**: unlimited paraphrasing length, all modes, faster processing, tone detection, and frozen "keep these words" controls

## Best For

Students, ESL and non-native English writers, and content creators who need to rephrase text for clarity, reduce repetition, tighten wordy passages, or shift a draft between formal and casual registers.

## Limitations

The free plan's word cap and locked modes push regular users toward Premium quickly. Heavier rewrites can flatten voice or subtly shift meaning, so output still needs a human read. And because paraphrasing works by swapping synonyms and reordering, it should not be treated as a way to dodge plagiarism or AI-detection checks.
