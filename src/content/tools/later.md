Later is a social media scheduling platform known for its visual, Instagram-first approach. Its core is a drag-and-drop content calendar and a media library where you plan a grid before it goes live — handy for accounts that care how the overall feed looks. It layers AI on top with a caption generator and hashtag suggestions, plus best-time-to-post recommendations drawn from your own audience data.

Compared with Buffer or Hootsuite, which are more text- and analytics-driven, Later leans into imagery and creator workflows. Its Link in Bio tool (originally Linkin.bio) is one of the more established alternatives to Linktree, letting you turn an Instagram or TikTok profile link into a clickable, shoppable landing page. That combination makes it popular with lifestyle brands, influencers, and small e-commerce shops.

## Key Features

- Visual drag-and-drop content calendar and media library
- Auto-publish to Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook, and more
- AI caption writer with hashtag suggestions
- Best-time-to-post recommendations per account
- Link in Bio shoppable landing page
- First-comment scheduling and basic analytics

## Pricing

- **Starter (~$25/month)**: One set of social profiles, limited monthly posts
- **Growth (~$45/month)**: More profiles, unlimited posts, added analytics
- **Advanced (~$80/month)**: Multiple brands and team seats
- Prices are billed higher month-to-month than annually; a limited free trial is usually available.

## Best For

Instagram and TikTok creators, lifestyle and product brands, and small marketing teams who plan visually and want scheduling plus a link-in-bio page in one place.

## Limitations

Post caps on the lower plans can bite if you publish daily across several accounts, and Later is noticeably weaker for X/Twitter and other text-first networks where Buffer or FeedHive fit better. Deeper analytics and reporting are reserved for the pricier tiers.
