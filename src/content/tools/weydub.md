Weydub is an AI localization studio that takes a finished video in one language and turns it into a ready-to-publish version in another. It handles the whole chain: OCR reads hard-coded subtitles and on-screen text, AI inpainting removes the original text cleanly from the frames, and the translated text is pasted back in matching position, size, and color. On the audio side it translates speech and generates AI dubbing — with voice cloning available — across 40+ languages. It is the international edition of GhostCut, built for onboarding and payments outside mainland China.

Beyond single videos, Weydub covers image translation for thumbnails and product graphics, batch processing for teams localizing content at volume, and an API for developers who want to wire localization into their own pipeline.

## Key Features

- Hard-subtitle translation: OCR, AI text removal, and re-layout of translated text in the original style
- Smart removal of on-screen text, subtitles, and watermarks via video inpainting
- End-to-end video translation and AI dubbing in 40+ languages, with voice cloning
- Image translation for graphics and thumbnails
- Batch workflows for high-volume localization
- API access for integrating localization into custom pipelines

## Pricing

There's a free tier with tight limits (short clips, 720P output) for trying the workflow. Paid usage is credit-based rather than a flat subscription — you buy credit packs that scale down in per-credit cost at higher volumes — plus a premium membership that unlocks longer videos, Video Remover Pro, ultra-realistic voices, and batch localization.

## Best For

Creators, ecommerce media teams, educators, and agencies republishing video content across languages — especially when the source footage has burned-in subtitles or on-screen text that needs to be cleanly replaced, not just captioned over.
