Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's brand for AI woven across its whole product line, built on OpenAI's GPT models (the consumer chat currently runs on GPT-4o-class models). It shows up in three main places: the free Copilot chat in Windows 11 and Edge, the paid Microsoft 365 Copilot that lives inside Office apps, and GitHub Copilot for developers. The naming can be confusing because "Copilot" means something slightly different in each surface.

What sets it apart from standalone ChatGPT is grounding. Microsoft 365 Copilot connects to the Microsoft Graph — your emails, documents, calendar, and Teams chats — so it can draft a reply that references last week's meeting or summarize a specific SharePoint file. Google's competing Gemini for Workspace does the same thing in Docs and Gmail; the choice usually comes down to which office suite your organization already runs.

The consumer side leans on Bing for live web search and DALL-E 3 (via Copilot Designer) for image generation.

## Key Features

- Copilot chat built into Windows 11 taskbar and Edge sidebar
- In-app assistance across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams
- Microsoft 365 Copilot grounding on your own files and email
- Copilot Designer image generation powered by DALL-E 3
- Real-time web answers via Bing with citations
- Copilot+ PC on-device features (Recall, live captions) on newer hardware

## Pricing

- **Free**: Copilot chat in Windows, Edge, and the mobile app
- **Copilot Pro (~$20/month)**: Priority access and Copilot in personal Office apps
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot (~$30/user/month)**: Enterprise Copilot across the suite, requires a qualifying 365 license

## Best For

Teams and individuals already living in Microsoft 365 who want AI drafting, summarizing, and data analysis without leaving Word, Excel, or Outlook.

## Limitations

The genuinely useful business version stacks on top of an existing 365 subscription, so total cost adds up fast. In practice the in-app suggestions vary in quality — Excel formula help can miss the mark, and results improve when your files are well organized. If you're not in the Microsoft ecosystem, plain ChatGPT or Claude often gives a better standalone chat experience for the money.
