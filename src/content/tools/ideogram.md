Ideogram is an AI image generator built around the one thing most rivals get wrong: text inside images. Where Midjourney and DALL-E often produce garbled letters, Ideogram reliably renders words, short phrases, and labels — which makes it the practical choice for logos, posters, book covers, ad creative, and social graphics where the wording has to be correct.

The company was founded in 2022 by former Google Brain researchers, including Mohammad Norouzi, several of whom worked on Google's Imagen model. Successive releases (Ideogram 2.0 and later 3.0) sharpened both prompt adherence and typographic accuracy. Beyond the model itself, Ideogram added a Canvas workspace and Magic Fill so you can extend, patch, or restyle parts of an image instead of rerolling the whole thing.

## Key Features

- Best-in-class rendering of text and typography inside generated images
- Magic Fill (inpainting) for localized edits and object removal
- Canvas for expanding, arranging, and combining images
- Describe — reverse-engineer a prompt from an uploaded image
- Style presets: realistic, design, 3D, anime, illustration
- Prompt enhancement (Magic Prompt) that fleshes out short prompts
- Daily free generations without a credit card

## Pricing

- **Free**: a set of priority plus slower generations each day
- **Basic (~$7/month)**: several hundred priority generations monthly
- **Plus (~$16/month)**: around 1,000 priority generations plus faster queue
- **Pro (~$48/month)**: highest monthly generation volume for heavy users

## Best For

Graphic designers, marketers, indie makers, and social media managers who need legible text baked into an image — logos, quote graphics, posters, and typography-led designs — without opening a separate editor to add the words.

## Limitations

For pure photorealism, painterly art, or the most cinematic looks, Midjourney and FLUX still tend to edge ahead. Very long blocks of text can still misspell or distort, especially at small sizes, and there is no built-in video generation, so Ideogram stays a still-image specialist.
