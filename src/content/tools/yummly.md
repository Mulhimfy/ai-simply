Yummly is a recipe discovery and meal-planning app owned by Whirlpool Corporation, which acquired it in 2017 to pair with its connected kitchen appliances. Its hook is personalization: as you rate recipes and set your diet, allergies, and preferred cuisines, its recommendation engine tunes what it surfaces from a large aggregated catalog of recipes pulled from food blogs and publishers.

Unlike a single-brand recipe site, Yummly acts as an aggregator and organizer. You save recipes from around the web into collections, filter aggressively by dietary need, and let the meal planner build a week of dinners that rolls up into a consolidated shopping list. Yummly Pro adds guided, step-by-step cooking with embedded video, and the app can send cooking instructions directly to compatible Whirlpool, Maytag, and KitchenAid smart ovens.

## Key Features

- Taste-profile personalization that improves as you rate recipes
- Detailed dietary, allergy, and cuisine filters
- Weekly meal planner with an auto-built shopping list
- Guided Cooking mode with step-by-step video (Pro)
- Recipe saving and collections from across the web
- Smart appliance integration with Whirlpool-family ovens

## Pricing

- **Free**: Recipe discovery, saving, and basic meal planning
- **Yummly Pro (~$4.99/month)**: Guided video cooking and an ad-free experience

## Best For

Home cooks who want recommendations tailored to their tastes, people cooking around allergies or a specific diet, and meal preppers who want planning and grocery lists handled in one place.

## Limitations

Because recipes are aggregated rather than tested in-house, instructions and quality vary by source. The best experiences — guided cooking and appliance control — assume you own compatible smart hardware, which most users don't. And the recommendation engine only gets useful after you've rated a fair number of recipes, so the first sessions feel generic compared with browsing a curated site.
