Looka is an AI-assisted logo and brand-identity generator (formerly Logojoy). You enter your business name, pick industry and style preferences, and rate a few sample logos; the tool then generates dozens of logo options you can refine in an editor — adjusting colors, fonts, layout, and symbols. It's aimed at founders and small businesses who need a presentable brand quickly and cheaply rather than a custom identity from an agency.

It competes with Canva's logo maker, Wix Logo Maker, Tailor Brands, and marketplaces like 99designs. Looka's niche is the balance of AI generation plus a full Brand Kit — once you have a logo, it produces matching business cards, social profiles, email signatures, and templates so the branding stays consistent across touchpoints.

## Key Features

- AI logo generation guided by your style and color preferences
- In-browser editor to fine-tune fonts, colors, and layout
- Brand Kit with hundreds of matching branded templates
- Business card, social media, and email signature assets
- High-resolution PNG plus vector SVG/EPS files
- Auto-generated brand color palette and font pairings
- Unlimited previews and revisions before purchase

## Pricing

- **Basic Logo (~$20 one-time)**: High-resolution logo files
- **Premium Logo (~$65 one-time)**: Full file set including vectors and variations
- **Brand Kit (~$96/year)**: Logo plus the full library of branded templates
- **Brand Kit Web (~$129+/year)**: Brand kit bundled with a website builder

## Best For

Entrepreneurs, startups, and small businesses that need a professional-looking logo and consistent brand assets fast and on a tight budget — without hiring a designer or running a design contest.

## Limitations

Because output is template-driven, logos can look generic and may resemble other Looka-made brands, so it's not a replacement for a distinctive custom identity. Everything is preview-only until you pay to download, and businesses that need a truly unique mark or deep brand strategy are better served by a professional designer or agency.
