Buffer is a long-running social media management platform focused on simplicity. You connect your accounts, draft posts, and queue them to publish automatically at set times across networks like Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Pinterest, and Bluesky. Its built-in AI Assistant can generate post ideas, write and rewrite captions, adjust tone, and repurpose a single post into platform-appropriate versions. It competes with Hootsuite, Later, and Sprout Social.

Buffer's calling card is being approachable and affordable. Where enterprise tools bury you in features, Buffer keeps the workflow clean, which makes it a favorite among solo creators, freelancers, and small businesses. It prices per connected channel rather than per big feature bundle, so a one-person brand pays very little, while agencies with dozens of accounts may find the model adds up.

Beyond scheduling, Buffer offers analytics with best-time-to-post suggestions, a landing-page builder (Start Page), and team approval workflows for collaborative publishing.

## Key Features

- Scheduling and auto-publishing across major social networks
- AI Assistant for captions, ideas, and repurposing
- Analytics with best-time-to-post recommendations
- Instagram, Reels, and Stories scheduling
- Start Page link-in-bio landing pages
- Team collaboration and post-approval workflows

## Pricing

- **Free**: Up to 3 channels with a limited queue of scheduled posts
- **Essentials (around $6/month per channel)**: Unlimited scheduling, analytics, and AI Assistant
- **Team (around $12/month per channel)**: Collaboration and approval tools
- **Agency**: Volume pricing for managing many client channels

Because billing is per channel, cost scales with the number of accounts you connect.

## Best For

Solo creators, freelancers, and small businesses who want a simple, affordable way to plan and schedule content across a handful of platforms without the complexity of enterprise suites.

## Limitations

Buffer's analytics and social-listening features are lighter than heavyweight tools like Sprout Social or Hootsuite, so data-driven teams may outgrow it. Per-channel pricing that looks cheap for one brand can get expensive across many accounts, and the AI Assistant, while handy, is a convenience rather than a reason to choose Buffer over a dedicated copywriting tool.
