Tome generates complete presentations from a single prompt. Describe a pitch deck, a product overview, or a sales proposal and it produces a full narrative — slides, copy, and images — that you then refine, rather than assembling from a blank canvas. Where PowerPoint and Google Slides start you with an empty grid, Tome starts you with a draft.

Its distinguishing feature is the interactive tile: instead of static screenshots, slides can embed live content like Figma frames, Loom videos, maps, and code. Combined with a mobile-friendly, scroll-style layout, that pushes Tome toward a more web-native format than traditional slideware, and closer in spirit to AI-deck rivals like Gamma and Beautiful.ai.

Worth knowing: Tome has increasingly repositioned around sales and go-to-market use cases over time, so the roadmap leans toward outbound and account workflows more than general-purpose slide design.

## Key Features

- AI generates a full presentation from a prompt in seconds
- Smart layouts that adapt automatically to your content
- Live tile embeds — Figma, Loom, maps, code, and more
- Built-in AI image generation for slide visuals
- Shareable web link for presenting without a download
- AI outline and rewrite tools for refining a draft

## Pricing

- **Free**: A starting allotment of AI credits with unlimited pages
- **Pro (~$16/month)**: Unlimited AI, custom fonts, and view analytics
- **Enterprise**: Custom branding, SSO, and admin controls

## Best For

Founders building pitch decks, sales teams assembling proposals, and anyone who needs a polished, on-brand presentation quickly without wrestling with slide design.

## Limitations

You trade precision for speed — Tome gives less pixel-level control than PowerPoint or Google Slides, so it's a poor fit if your organization requires exact corporate templates or complex custom layouts. Its shift toward sales workflows also means general presentation features get less focus than they once did. For maximum design control, traditional slideware still wins; for pure AI-deck speed, Gamma is a close alternative.
