Notion AI is a set of AI features woven into Notion, the popular all-in-one workspace for notes, docs, wikis, and databases. Instead of switching to a separate chatbot, you invoke the AI right inside a page to draft content, summarize a long doc, fix grammar, change tone, or translate — and its standout feature, Q&A, can search and answer questions across your whole workspace, turning a company's Notion into a queryable knowledge base.

The advantage is integration: the AI works where your information already lives, so answers can draw on your actual notes, projects, and wikis rather than generic training data. That is also the trade-off — Notion AI is tuned for productivity inside Notion, not for the open-ended, heavy-duty generation you'd get from ChatGPT or Claude directly. It competes less with those chatbots head-on and more with other embedded assistants like Coda AI or Slack AI.

## Key Features

- Write, summarize, rewrite, and change tone within any page
- Q&A across your entire workspace knowledge base
- Autofill database properties with AI
- Meeting-note capture and summarization
- Translation across many languages
- Works natively alongside all Notion docs, wikis, and databases

## Pricing

- **Add-on (~$8-10 per member/month)**: adds AI to any existing Notion plan
- **Requires a Notion plan underneath**: free, Plus, Business, or Enterprise as the base
- Some newer plans bundle a level of AI usage into the subscription

## Best For

Teams and individuals already committed to Notion — especially those using it as a company wiki, project hub, and documentation home — who want AI assistance and workspace-wide search without leaving the tool.

## Limitations

Notion AI is billed per member on top of your existing subscription, so the cost scales with team size. For long-form or specialized generation it is weaker than dedicated models, and Q&A can feel slow or return shallow answers on very large, messy workspaces. If your notes do not already live in Notion, a standalone assistant makes more sense.
