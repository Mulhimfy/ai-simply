Pi (short for "personal intelligence") is a conversational AI from Inflection AI, the startup co-founded by DeepMind co-founder Mustafa Suleyman and LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman. It launched in 2023 with a deliberately different goal from ChatGPT: instead of maximizing task output, Pi was designed to be a kind, emotionally intelligent companion for talking things through.

That focus shows in how it behaves. Pi asks follow-up questions, mirrors your tone, and paces a conversation rather than dumping a wall of text. Its voice mode is one of the most natural available, with several distinct voice options that sound calm and unhurried. You can use it on the web, iOS, Android, and through WhatsApp and other messaging channels.

One important caveat: in March 2024 Microsoft hired much of Inflection's team and Suleyman, and the company shifted toward selling its models to enterprises. Pi still runs, but its long-term roadmap is less certain than that of ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

## Key Features

- Empathetic conversational style built for reflection and support
- Natural voice mode with multiple selectable voices
- Available on web, iOS, Android, and WhatsApp
- Remembers context within and across sessions
- Suggested replies to keep a conversation flowing
- No sign-in wall to start chatting

## Pricing

- **Free**: Full conversational access with no advertised usage caps or paid tier

## Best For

People who want a patient, judgment-free AI to think out loud with — processing a hard day, weighing a decision, or practicing a conversation — rather than a productivity assistant for writing code or crunching documents.

## Limitations

Pi is intentionally narrow. It has no file upload, no live web browsing, and no coding or spreadsheet tooling, so for real work ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini are far more capable. It is also not a substitute for professional mental-health care. And given Inflection's enterprise pivot, there is real uncertainty about how much the consumer product will keep improving.
