Wanderlog is a trip-planning app that pulls the scattered parts of travel organization — a route map, a day-by-day itinerary, restaurant and attraction lists, and reservation confirmations — into one place. You can drop places onto an interactive map, group them by day, and let the app optimize the driving or walking order so you are not zigzagging across a city. It layers on AI suggestions for things to do based on your destination and interests.

Compared with TripIt (which focuses narrowly on organizing bookings) or Google Maps lists (which lack real itinerary structure), Wanderlog's strength is the combination of a strong map, structured multi-day planning, and real-time collaboration — several people can edit the same trip at once, making it popular for group travel. It also parses forwarded confirmation emails into a tidy reservation timeline.

## Key Features

- Interactive map plotting every saved place, with route optimization
- Day-by-day itinerary builder
- AI suggestions for attractions, restaurants, and activities
- Real-time collaborative editing for group trips
- Automatic import of flight/hotel confirmations from email
- Reservation and budget tracking
- Offline access to maps and plans (Pro)

## Pricing

- **Free**: unlimited trips and most core planning features, with ads
- **Pro (~$50/year)**: offline access, ad-free, unlimited itinerary optimization, expense tracking, and premium extras

The free tier is unusually capable, so many travelers never need to upgrade.

## Best For

Independent and group travelers who want one organized home for a detailed, map-based itinerary — especially anyone coordinating a multi-stop trip with friends or family.

## Limitations

The AI itinerary generator gives a decent starting draft but typically needs hand-editing to match real preferences and pacing. Wanderlog also doesn't book flights or hotels itself — it links out to booking sites — and several of its best conveniences, like offline maps and an ad-free experience, sit behind the Pro subscription.
