FeedHive is an AI-assisted social media scheduler aimed at solo creators and personal brands, with a focus on writing and timing text-first posts. Its headline feature is Performance Prediction: before you publish, an AI model trained on engagement patterns estimates how well a post is likely to do, so you can tweak wording or timing first. An AI writing assistant helps draft and reword posts with tone control.

Where it differs from visual-led tools like Later is its automation. Content recycling reposts your best evergreen content on a schedule, and conditional posting can trigger a follow-up automatically — for example, adding a comment with a link only if a post passes an engagement threshold. Those workflows make it especially popular with X/Twitter and LinkedIn creators who post frequently and want to squeeze more from each piece.

## Key Features

- AI Performance Prediction scoring before you publish
- AI writing assistant with tone and rewrite controls
- Content recycling and evergreen queues
- Conditional/auto-posting based on performance triggers
- Hashtag and @mention manager
- Multi-account scheduling with team collaboration

## Pricing

- **Creator (~$19/month)**: Core AI features and prediction for a handful of profiles
- **Brand (~$29/month)**: More posts, recycling, and profiles
- **Business/Agency (from ~$99/month)**: Higher limits and multiple clients
- A free trial is usually available; annual billing lowers the monthly rate.

## Best For

Solo creators, founders, consultants, and personal brands growing on X/Twitter and LinkedIn who want AI help writing posts, plus recycling and prediction to get more mileage from their content.

## Limitations

Performance prediction is a directional signal based on patterns, not a guarantee — treat it as a nudge rather than a rule. FeedHive is also lighter on visual grid planning than Later, and its pricing and feature focus skew toward individuals and small teams rather than large agencies.
