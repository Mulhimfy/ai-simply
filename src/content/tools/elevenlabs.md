ElevenLabs is a text-to-speech and voice-AI company, founded in 2022 by former Google and Palantir engineers Piotr Dabkowski and Mati Staniszewski, that is widely considered the current leader in realistic AI speech. Its models are prized for catching the small human details — breath, emphasis, emotion, and pacing — that make earlier text-to-speech sound robotic.

The platform does more than read text aloud. You can clone a voice from a short recording (Instant Voice Cloning) or build a higher-fidelity Professional clone, browse thousands of community and pre-built voices in a marketplace, and use Dubbing to translate audio or video into other languages while keeping the original speaker's voice. There are also tools for long-form audio (audiobooks/Projects), sound-effects generation, and a conversational-AI/agents product for building voice bots.

Against rivals like Murf, Play.ht, and cloud TTS from Google and Amazon, ElevenLabs competes almost entirely on raw quality and expressiveness, backed by a strong developer API. The trade-off is a character-based pricing model that adds up for heavy, long-form use.

## Key Features

- Highly natural, expressive text-to-speech across many languages
- Instant and Professional voice cloning
- Voice marketplace with thousands of voices
- Dubbing that preserves the original voice across languages
- Long-form audio / audiobook production tools
- Sound-effects generation from text prompts
- Conversational AI agents and a full developer API

## Pricing

- **Free**: A modest monthly character allowance for testing, non-commercial
- **Starter (~$USD 5/month)**: More characters plus Instant Voice Cloning
- **Creator (~$USD 22/month)**: Larger character pool and higher-quality audio
- **Pro (~$USD 99/month)**: Much higher limits with commercial usage rights
- **Scale / Business / Enterprise**: Higher volume tiers and custom API terms

Billing is by characters generated, so long audiobooks or high-volume apps move through allotments quickly, and the top-quality models cost more characters.

## Best For

Podcasters, video creators, audiobook publishers, game and app developers, and localization teams who need the most lifelike AI voices — especially anyone dubbing content into multiple languages or building voice features via API.

## Limitations

The character-based pricing is the main catch: long-form or high-volume production can get costly, and premium-quality models eat the budget faster. Voice cloning also carries ethical and legal weight — cloning a real person's voice requires their consent, and misuse (impersonation, fraud) is a genuine risk the whole industry is grappling with. For simple, budget narration synced to slides, a studio tool like Murf may be more cost-effective.
