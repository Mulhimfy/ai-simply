XlearnAPI is an API aggregation platform that puts a single OpenAI-compatible endpoint in front of a lot of different model providers. Swap your BaseURL, keep the request format you already use, and call GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen, Moonshot, or Zhipu without maintaining a separate integration and billing relationship for each. Coverage extends past text: Midjourney for images, Suno for music, and Runway, Kling, and MiniMax Hailuo for video are all reachable through the same interface.

Because it speaks standard OpenAI format, existing clients and orchestration tools plug in without code changes — LobeChat, ChatGPT-Next-Web, and OpenCat on the app side, Dify, FastGPT, MaxKB, Coze, Make, and Zapier on the agent and automation side. The platform runs smart load balancing across regional nodes to keep latency and retry rates down, and publishes a 99.9% availability SLA with 24/7 support.

Worth knowing before you commit: the site and dashboard are primarily Chinese-language, and support runs through WeChat as well as email.

## Key Features

- One OpenAI-compatible BaseURL for GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen, Moonshot, and Zhipu
- Image, video, and music models alongside text — Midjourney, Suno, Runway, Kling, MiniMax Hailuo
- Drop-in compatibility with LobeChat, ChatGPT-Next-Web, OpenCat, and any OpenAI-format client
- Agent and automation integrations for Dify, FastGPT, MaxKB, Coze, Make, and Zapier
- Smart load balancing across global nodes with high-concurrency handling
- Pay-as-you-go credit billing instead of per-provider subscriptions
- 99.9% uptime SLA, 24/7 technical support, and a 30-day refund window
- Enterprise tier with custom SLA, invoicing, and private deployment

## Pricing

Paid, credit-based top-ups with no subscription required — you load a balance and spend it per call. Entry point is $5 (¥19.99), with the platform claiming roughly 50% savings versus calling the official APIs directly. An Enterprise plan starts around $140/month (¥999) and adds a dedicated account manager, custom SLA and contracts, invoicing, and optional Docker or cloud private deployment. No credit card is required to register.

## Best For

Developers and small AI teams who want to test or ship against several model providers without juggling separate accounts, keys, and payment methods for each — particularly anyone building on OpenAI-format tooling who wants to swap models by changing a string.
