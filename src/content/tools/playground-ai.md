Playground (formerly Playground AI) is a browser-based image generation and editing tool built around an infinite canvas rather than a single prompt box. You place, resize, and layer generated images on a workspace that feels closer to Figma or Canva than to a chat-style generator, then refine any region with inpainting and outpainting. The pitch is volume and control: a large free daily allowance plus editing tools aimed at people who want to iterate, not just generate one-off pictures.

Compared to Midjourney, which lives in Discord and leans toward stylized, painterly output, Playground targets designers who want to composite and edit directly. Against DALL-E inside ChatGPT, it offers more manual control — masks, model choice, and a canvas — at the cost of a steeper learning curve. It supports Stable Diffusion-based models and preset filters, so results vary by the engine you pick.

## Key Features

- Infinite canvas for placing, layering, and compositing images
- Inpainting to regenerate a masked area and outpainting to extend a frame
- Large free daily allowance on standard models
- Multiple base models including Stable Diffusion variants and community filters
- Text-to-image plus image-to-image reference workflows
- Style presets and filters for consistent looks across a set

## Pricing

- **Free**: A high daily image count on standard models, with a smaller daily cap on premium models
- **Pro (around $12-$15/month)**: Higher premium-model limits and faster queue priority
- Higher tiers add more premium generations and commercial-use terms

## Best For

Designers, marketers, and hobbyists who want to generate and then edit images in one place, and anyone who needs high daily volume without paying per image. It suits people comfortable with layers and masks more than those who just want one polished result from a prompt.

## Limitations

Photorealism and coherence still trail Midjourney, and the most capable models are metered even on paid plans, so heavy users hit caps. The product iterates quickly, so exact limits, model names, and prices shift — check the current plan page before committing.
