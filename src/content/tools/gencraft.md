Gencraft is a consumer AI art app built for phones. You type a prompt, pick from a menu of preset styles — oil painting, anime, 3D render, photorealism, cyberpunk and more — and it generates images in seconds. The whole experience is designed around casual, on-the-go creation rather than the fine-grained control that power users chase, which makes it an easy entry point for people new to AI art.

It sits in the same casual-mobile category as apps like Wonder and Dawn AI, competing on breadth of styles and speed rather than raw output quality. That is the main trade-off against desktop-oriented tools such as Midjourney or Krea: Gencraft is friendlier and faster to start, but gives you less say over composition, seeds, and fine detail. It is available on the web, iOS, and Android.

## Key Features

- Text-to-image generation with dozens of style presets
- Image-to-image and style transfer from a photo
- Short AI video/animation generation from images
- Mobile-first apps for iOS and Android plus web access
- Prompt gallery and community feed for inspiration
- HD/upscaled output on paid tiers

## Pricing

- **Free**: A small number of generations per day
- **Pro (~$10/month)**: Unlimited or high-volume generations and all styles
- **Ultimate (~$20/month)**: Faster/priority generation and higher-resolution output
- Plans are typically billed as recurring subscriptions through the app stores.

## Best For

Casual creators, social media users, and hobbyists who want quick, varied AI artwork from their phone without learning prompt engineering or installing desktop software.

## Limitations

Output quality and control lag behind tools like Midjourney and Krea, so it is not the pick for professional-grade renders. Like many app-store subscription tools, its auto-renewing billing draws complaints, and the reliance on presets means results can look generic if you want a very specific style.
