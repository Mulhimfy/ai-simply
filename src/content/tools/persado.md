Persado is an enterprise AI platform focused on marketing language. Rather than a general-purpose writer, it specializes in generating and optimizing message copy — subject lines, calls to action, push notifications, and body text — chosen for the emotional framing most likely to drive a response from a given audience. The system draws on a large, tagged corpus of real campaign outcomes, so its recommendations are tied to what has actually performed rather than to fluency alone.

That performance-data foundation is the key difference from tools like Jasper, Copy.ai, or Writer, which prioritize speed and volume of on-brand content. Persado is aimed at large brands running high-volume campaigns where a small lift in click or conversion rate is worth real money, and where every message must clear brand and regulatory review. It's commonly used by banks, insurers, retailers, and travel companies.

Persado was founded in 2012 and sells exclusively to enterprises, typically integrating with existing marketing and CRM stacks.

## Key Features

- AI-generated message variants optimized for emotional impact
- Recommendations grounded in a large database of campaign results
- Coverage of email, SMS, push, web, and ad copy
- Predicted performance and A/B guidance before launch
- Audience- and segment-level personalization of language
- Brand-safety and compliance guardrails for regulated sectors

## Pricing

- **Enterprise**: Custom, quote-based pricing negotiated with sales; no public self-serve or free tier

## Best For

Large marketing and CRM teams — especially in financial services, retail, and travel — that run high-volume messaging and want copy optimized for measurable conversion lift rather than just fast content generation.

## Limitations

Persado is strictly an enterprise product, so there's no way to try it cheaply and no transparent pricing. The economics only make sense at scale, where marginal lifts justify the cost and a real experimentation program can measure them. Small teams are far better served by self-serve writers like Jasper or Copy.ai.
