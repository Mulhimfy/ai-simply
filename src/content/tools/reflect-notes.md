Reflect is a note-taking app built around networked thought — the idea, popularized by Roam Research and Obsidian, that notes become more useful when they link to each other. Reflect's pitch is that it delivers that backlinked, graph-style thinking without the fiddly setup and steep learning curve those tools are known for.

The app centers on daily notes: you write into a running dated journal, mention people or ideas with `[[` links, and Reflect automatically builds the connections and surfaces backlinks. An AI assistant (built on GPT models) can continue a thought, summarize a note, fix grammar, or pull out action items. It also connects to your calendar to pre-create meeting notes and can transcribe voice memos into text.

Two things set it apart from the Notion/Obsidian crowd: everything is end-to-end encrypted, and the whole product is deliberately minimal — fast, keyboard-driven, and opinionated rather than infinitely configurable.

## Key Features

- Networked notes with automatic backlinks and a graph view
- Daily notes with automatic date-linking
- GPT-based AI assistant for summaries, rewriting, and action items
- Calendar integration that pre-builds meeting notes
- Voice memo transcription
- End-to-end encryption across devices
- Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android apps

## Pricing

- **Trial**: Free time-limited trial to test the app
- **Personal (~$10/month, billed annually)**: Unlimited notes, AI assistant, all integrations
- Discounted annual billing; no permanent free tier

## Best For

Knowledge workers, founders, writers, and consultants who think by writing and want lightweight networked notes plus AI help connecting ideas across meetings and journals — without the configuration overhead of Obsidian or the sprawl of Notion.

## Limitations

There is no free tier, so it competes on price against Obsidian (free for personal use) and Notion (generous free plan). The ecosystem of plugins, templates, and community extensions is much smaller than either. And if you only need simple lists or a basic notepad, the backlink-and-graph model is more structure than the job requires.
