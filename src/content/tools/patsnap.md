PatSnap is an intellectual property intelligence platform aimed at R&D leaders, innovation managers, and patent professionals. Founded in Singapore and now operating globally, it aggregates hundreds of millions of patents plus scientific literature, and layers AI-driven search and analytics on top so users can explore a technology field rather than hunt one document at a time.

Its differentiator is accessibility. Traditional patent databases like Derwent or free tools like Google Patents assume you can write precise Boolean and classification queries. PatSnap leans on semantic and natural-language search, so an engineer can describe an idea in plain terms and get relevant prior art, then visualize the surrounding landscape — who owns what, where the crowded areas are, and where "white space" exists for new innovation.

Beyond search, it supports competitor portfolio tracking, patent family and citation analysis, freedom-to-operate research, and specialized chemical structure and biological sequence search. More recently PatSnap has added generative-AI assistants (its Hero/Eureka line) to summarize patents and answer innovation questions in natural language.

## Key Features

- Semantic, natural-language patent and literature search across 100+ jurisdictions
- Technology landscape and white-space visualization
- Competitor patent portfolio and trend tracking
- Patent family, legal status, and citation analysis
- Chemical structure and biological sequence search
- AI assistants that summarize patents and surface insights

## Pricing

- **Professional / Team**: Subscription based, quoted by seats and modules — contact sales
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing with API access, higher volume, and advanced analytics

Pricing is not published; expect a paid annual contract rather than a self-serve plan.

## Best For

R&D and innovation teams scoping new technology areas, patent attorneys running prior-art and freedom-to-operate searches, and strategy teams monitoring competitors' IP and emerging technology trends.

## Limitations

Pricing is quote-only and generally sits at the enterprise level, so it is poor value for anyone who just needs occasional lookups — Google Patents or Espacenet cover that for free. The breadth of data and analytics also means a learning curve, and getting full value usually requires committing to the platform as a core research workflow.
