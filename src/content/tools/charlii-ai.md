Charlii AI ("AI博士Charlii") is a knowledge site rather than a piece of software — a running library of practical AI guides aimed at people who want to actually put models to work rather than read about them. The bulk of it is workflow content: how knowledge workers structure an AI workflow end to end, how small teams pick tools for collaboration and documentation, how to use AI for competitive analysis and content planning, and how to summarize research material without losing sources and detail.

Alongside the workflow pieces sits a steady stream of head-to-head tool comparisons — Claude for long-form writing, ChatGPT for content marketing, the best AI note-taking tools, research tools for source management — plus a prompt library, AI news, and summaries of new papers and model releases. Technical posts go deeper when warranted: local deployment of DeepSeek-R1, the MCP protocol, open-source Manus alternatives like OWL and OpenHands, batch API cost optimization.

One practical note: the site is primarily Chinese-language, though a growing share of articles are marked bilingual.

## Key Features

- Step-by-step AI workflow guides for knowledge workers and small teams
- Head-to-head tool comparisons and "best of" roundups by task
- Prompt engineering library organized by use case
- AI news feed plus summaries of research papers and model releases
- Technical tutorials on local model deployment, MCP, and API cost optimization
- Email newsletter with new tutorials, tool breakdowns, and workflow templates

## Pricing

The blog, guides, prompt library, and newsletter are free. The site also offers paid consultation and procurement services — Claude Code and GPT credit top-ups, team purchasing, and long-term arrangements — handled manually rather than through automated checkout, with pricing confirmed case by case.

## Best For

Founders, operators, and small teams who want applied AI guidance — which tool for which job, and what the workflow around it looks like — rather than model benchmarks or general AI commentary. Most useful to Chinese-reading audiences given the site's primary language.
