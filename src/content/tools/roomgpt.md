RoomGPT lets you upload a photo of a room and see it reimagined in a chosen interior-design style — modern, minimalist, Scandinavian, industrial, and more — in a few seconds. It uses generative image models to repaint the space while keeping the rough layout, giving homeowners, renters, and agents a fast way to picture alternatives before spending money on furniture or renovation.

Its origin story is part of the appeal: RoomGPT started as a popular open-source project by Hassan El Mghari, a developer at Vercel, and became one of the more widely cloned AI demo apps. That heritage keeps it lightweight and focused on a single job — restyling — rather than the broader workflows of tools like Interior AI, REimagine Home, or Planner 5D, which push toward staging, 3D layouts, or shoppable product matches.

Because it's built on diffusion image models, RoomGPT excels at mood and style exploration but doesn't produce measured, buildable designs.

## Key Features

- Photo upload with AI restyling in seconds
- A range of interior styles (modern, rustic, industrial, minimalist, and more)
- Support for several room types — bedroom, kitchen, living room, office
- Before/after comparison of original and redesign
- High-resolution downloads on paid credits
- Simple, no-frills interface with quick onboarding

## Pricing

- **Free**: A small number of trial generations to start
- **Paid**: Credit packs and low-cost monthly plans (roughly $10–$30 range) that unlock more renders and remove watermarks

## Best For

Homeowners weighing a redecoration, renters wanting ideas that don't touch the structure, and real-estate agents who want quick virtual restaging to help buyers imagine a space.

## Limitations

The output is inspiration, not a plan you can hand to a contractor — there are no measurements, product lists, or guaranteed-realistic layouts, and the model sometimes warps windows, doors, or proportions. Anyone needing accurate 3D layouts or shoppable furniture should look at fuller suites like Planner 5D or Interior AI.
