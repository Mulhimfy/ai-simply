Tweet Hunter is a growth suite for Twitter/X built by Lempire, the company behind lemlist and the LinkedIn tool Taplio. It packages three things creators usually juggle separately — an AI writer, a scheduler, and a relationship tracker — into one dashboard aimed at people trying to build an audience or generate leads on X.

The standout is its inspiration library: a searchable database of high-performing tweets you can filter by topic and remix, with AI helping rewrite them in your voice. Compared with general schedulers like Buffer or Hypefury, Tweet Hunter leans harder into the "study what went viral, then replicate the structure" playbook, plus automation features like auto-DMing users who like a specific tweet and auto-plugging a follow-up on threads that take off.

Because it sits on top of the X API, its automation features are subject to whatever X allows and charges at any given time.

## Key Features

- AI tweet and thread writer trained on high-performing posts
- Searchable viral tweet library filtered by niche
- Visual scheduler with evergreen queue and auto-retweet
- Auto-DM triggered when someone likes a chosen tweet
- Auto-plug that appends a CTA to threads once they gain traction
- Built-in CRM and analytics on your top content

## Pricing

- **No free tier**: Paid only, typically with a short trial
- **Starter (~$49/month)**: AI writing, scheduling, analytics
- **Team/Pro (~$99+/month)**: Auto-engagement, CRM, and collaboration features

Pricing shifts with plan bundling and X API costs, so check the current tiers before subscribing.

## Best For

Solo creators, indie founders, and B2B marketers who post frequently on X and want to speed up writing, keep an evergreen queue running, and turn engagement into DMs and leads.

## Limitations

There's no free plan, so it only pays off if you post seriously. The auto-DM and auto-engagement tools can feel spammy to followers and risk running afoul of X's rules if pushed too hard. And since everything rides on X's API — which has become costly and unpredictable — features can change or break outside Tweet Hunter's control. For simple scheduling alone, a cheaper tool will do.
