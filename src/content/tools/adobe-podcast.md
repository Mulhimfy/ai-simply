Adobe Podcast is a suite of web-based AI audio tools from Adobe, best known for Enhance Speech — a feature that takes a rough recording and strips out background noise, room echo, and hum while boosting vocal clarity, so a clip captured on a laptop or phone mic can sound closer to a studio microphone. It runs in the browser with no software install and, for basic use, is free.

Enhance Speech is the headline. It became widely shared because the before-and-after difference is dramatic on noisy or reverberant audio, rescuing recordings that would otherwise be unusable. Alongside it, Mic Check analyzes your microphone and room in real time before you hit record, flagging issues like distance, background noise, or clipping, and the platform includes transcript-based editing so you can cut audio by deleting words in the text.

Its closest comparisons are Krisp and Descript (which also does transcript-based editing) and cleanup tools like iZotope RX. Adobe's edge is that Enhance Speech is free, browser-based, and remarkably simple, tied into Adobe's broader Creative Cloud ecosystem.

## Key Features

- Enhance Speech — one-click noise, echo, and reverb removal with voice clarity boost
- Mic Check — real-time analysis of your mic and recording environment
- Transcript-based editing (edit audio by editing text)
- Browser-based recording and editing, no download required
- AI features for cleaning up interviews, podcasts, and voiceovers
- Integration with Adobe's Creative Cloud tools

## Pricing

- **Free**: Enhance Speech and core tools in the browser, with a monthly cap on how much audio you can process
- **Higher limits**: Expanded access via Adobe accounts / Creative Cloud, with paid Adobe plans lifting usage caps

Adobe periodically adjusts limits and packaging; check the site for current allowances.

## Best For

Podcasters, YouTubers, remote and hybrid workers, and anyone recording spoken audio on modest equipment who needs to quickly clean up noise and echo without learning audio engineering or buying expensive gear.

## Limitations

Enhance Speech works best on spoken voice; pushed hard or used on tricky recordings, it can leave voices sounding processed, thin, or slightly "underwater," and it is not meant for music or complex multi-track mixes. The free tier also caps how much audio you can enhance, so heavy or professional users may hit limits or turn to dedicated tools like iZotope RX or Descript for finer control.
