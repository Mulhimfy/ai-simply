Writesonic is an AI writing platform built around SEO and marketing content. Its centerpiece is the AI Article Writer, which researches a keyword, builds an outline, and drafts a long-form post, plus Chatsonic — a ChatGPT-style assistant with live web search so answers reflect current events rather than a fixed training cutoff. It sits in the same space as Jasper and Copy.ai but leans harder into search-ranking workflows.

What separates Writesonic from a plain chatbot is the surrounding toolkit: keyword research, an SEO checker, and an integration with SurferSEO that scores drafts against the pages already ranking for your target term. That makes it a fit for teams who care less about clever prose and more about publishing content that Google will actually index and rank.

The platform is owned by the company of the same name and also ships Botsonic, a no-code builder for customer-support chatbots trained on your own site data.

## Key Features

- AI Article Writer that researches, outlines, and drafts long-form posts
- Chatsonic assistant with real-time Google search and citations
- SEO checker and keyword research tools
- SurferSEO integration for content scoring
- Bulk generation and CSV upload for producing many pieces at once
- Landing page, ad, and product-description templates
- Botsonic add-on for building support chatbots

## Pricing

- **Free**: Limited trial words to test the platform
- **Individual (around $16/month)**: Core writing and Chatsonic access with a monthly credit allowance
- **Standard / Business (higher tiers)**: More credits, team seats, brand voice, and API access
- **Enterprise**: Custom volume pricing and dedicated support

Writesonic changes its plan names and limits often, so confirm current credit allowances on the pricing page before committing.

## Best For

Content marketers, bloggers, agencies, and SEO teams who publish frequently and want research, drafting, and optimization in one tool rather than stitching together a chatbot and a separate SEO app.

## Limitations

The credit system is the main friction — sustained daily publishing can push you into higher tiers quickly. Drafts also read as generic without human editing, and for pure conversational quality a dedicated model like ChatGPT or Claude will usually feel sharper than Chatsonic.
