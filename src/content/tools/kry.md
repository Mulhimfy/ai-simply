K Health is a US telehealth app that pairs an AI symptom checker with licensed clinicians. The AI is trained on a large database of real, de-identified patient records, so instead of generic web advice it tells you how people with a similar profile and symptoms were diagnosed and treated. You then connect to a real physician or nurse practitioner who reviews the assessment and can prescribe medication where appropriate.

The AI-plus-clinician model is what separates K Health from a pure symptom checker like Ada or WebMD, which stop at information, and from telehealth services like Teladoc that connect you straight to a doctor without the data-driven pre-visit. K Health has also partnered with major health systems (including Cedars-Sinai and Mayo Clinic) to bring the approach into established care networks, and it spans primary care, mental health, and chronic-condition management such as hypertension and diabetes.

## Key Features

- AI symptom check benchmarked against millions of real patient cases
- Licensed clinician review and virtual consultation
- E-prescriptions sent to your pharmacy
- Ongoing chronic care for conditions like hypertension and diabetes
- Mental health support, including medication management
- Chat-based visits with a saved health history

## Pricing

- **Free**: The AI symptom checker and general health information
- **Per-visit (around $35 one-time)**: A single clinician consultation
- **Membership (around $49/month)**: Unlimited primary care visits and messaging
- **Specialized programs**: Additional pricing for mental health or chronic care

## Best For

People in the US who want fast, affordable access to primary care and prescriptions for common, non-emergency issues — and those managing an ongoing condition who value quick messaging with a clinician instead of scheduling in-person visits.

## Limitations

K Health is US-only and unsuitable for emergencies or complex conditions that need a physical exam or in-person testing. The AI output is informational until a clinician confirms it, and full access still involves a per-visit fee or monthly membership, so it complements rather than fully replaces traditional care.
