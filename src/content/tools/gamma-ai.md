Gamma generates presentations, documents, and simple websites from a text prompt or an imported file. Type a topic or paste an outline, pick a theme, and it produces a full deck — structure, copy, layouts, icons, and stock or AI-generated images — in well under a minute. Founded in 2020 by former Optimizely product lead Grant Lee, Gamma became one of the breakout AI productivity apps, passing tens of millions of users while staying a small team.

What separates it from Tome (which pivoted away from presentations), Beautiful.ai, or Canva's slide tools is the "card" format. Gamma decks are flexible cards that can expand and scroll rather than fixed 16:9 slides, so the same content works as a shareable webpage, an embedded doc, or a traditional exported deck. Restyling is one click: change theme and every card re-flows, which kills the usual slide-by-slide font surgery.

The AI editing loop matters as much as generation. You can select any card and tell the AI to shorten it, split it in two, change the visual, or adjust tone — closer to editing with a designer than fighting a template.

## Key Features

- Generate a complete deck, doc, or webpage from a prompt or pasted outline
- Import Word, PowerPoint, or Notion content and restyle it automatically
- One-click theme changes that re-flow every card
- AI image generation built in, plus embeds for video, GIFs, charts, and websites
- Per-card AI editing — rewrite, condense, or redesign a single section
- Share as a live link with analytics on who viewed which cards
- Export to PDF and PPTX when a traditional file is required

## Pricing

- **Free**: ~400 AI credits at signup, unlimited manual editing, Gamma branding on shares
- **Plus (~$8-10/month)**: More monthly credits, removes branding, unlocks longer generations
- **Pro (~$15-20/month)**: Unlimited AI generation, priority models, custom fonts and advanced export

## Best For

Founders assembling pitch decks, marketers and consultants producing client-facing material on deadline, teachers building lesson decks, and anyone who dreads blank-slide syndrome and cares more about speed than pixel control.

## Limitations

AI actions consume credits, and free-tier credits do not renew generously, so heavy users end up on Pro. PPTX export sometimes shifts layouts, since cards do not map perfectly onto fixed slides — if your workflow ends in PowerPoint, expect cleanup. Precision designers will still prefer Figma or Keynote.
