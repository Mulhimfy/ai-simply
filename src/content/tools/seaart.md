SeaArt is a web-based AI art generator built on Stable Diffusion, aimed at users who want the flexibility of the open-source model ecosystem without installing anything. Instead of running AUTOMATIC1111 or ComfyUI locally, you pick from a large in-app library of community checkpoints and LoRAs and generate directly in the browser. Its strength is anime, fantasy, and character art, which has made it popular with illustrators, VTubers, and game and D&D hobbyists.

It sits alongside tools like Tensor.Art, Leonardo.Ai, and Civitai's on-site generator — hosted front ends for the Stable Diffusion world. SeaArt's draw is a genuinely usable free tier (daily credits), plus editing tools and a community feed where users share models, prompts, and images. For someone who wants Stable Diffusion's variety but not its setup, it lowers the barrier considerably.

## Key Features

- Stable Diffusion generation with swappable community models
- Large catalog of checkpoints and LoRAs for specific styles
- Anime, fantasy, realistic, and character-art specialties
- Inpainting, outpainting, and image-editing tools
- Batch generation for multiple variations at once
- Community gallery for sharing prompts and models

## Pricing

- **Free**: A daily allotment of credits (enough for a batch of images per day)
- **Standard**: Around $10/month for a larger monthly credit pool
- **Pro**: Around $20/month for higher credits and faster/priority generation

## Best For

Anime and fantasy artists, tabletop and game hobbyists, and Stable Diffusion fans who want access to a big community model library and free daily generations without configuring local software.

## Limitations

The free tier is metered by credits and can slow to a queue during peak demand, so heavy users end up on a paid plan. Output quality swings a lot depending on the community model and prompt you choose, and moderation of models and generated content can be uneven. Power users who want full control over pipelines will still prefer a local Stable Diffusion setup.
