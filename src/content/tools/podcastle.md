Podcastle is a browser-based, all-in-one platform for recording, editing, and publishing podcasts and voice content without a studio or desktop software. Its Magic Dust feature enhances audio in one click — reducing background noise, evening out levels, and making inexpensive microphones sound cleaner — and it transcribes episodes automatically for show notes and captions.

Its closest comparisons are Descript, Adobe Podcast, and Riverside. Like Descript, Podcastle offers text-based editing, where you trim audio by deleting words in the transcript instead of dragging waveforms. Like Riverside, it records remote guests on separate high-quality tracks. Podcastle's pitch is doing all of that in one accessible web app aimed at solo creators and small teams rather than professional audio engineers.

## Key Features

- One-click AI audio enhancement (Magic Dust) for noise and leveling
- Browser-based recording — nothing to install
- Automatic transcription and show notes
- Text-based editing: edit audio by editing the transcript
- Multi-track remote recording with guests on separate tracks
- Revoice AI voice cloning for corrections and text-to-speech

## Pricing

- **Free**: A few hours of recording per month with basic features
- **Storyteller (around $12/month)**: More recording hours and full AI features
- **Pro (around $24/month)**: Higher or unlimited limits and team features

## Best For

Solo podcasters, YouTubers, and small teams who want to produce clean, professional-sounding audio without buying gear or learning a full digital audio workstation. The remote recording and text editing suit interview-style shows.

## Limitations

Podcastle lacks the granular control of a dedicated DAW, and its AI enhancement can sound artificial when applied to audio that was already good. Free-tier hour and export limits push regular creators to a paid plan, and audio professionals may prefer Descript or a traditional editor for finer control.
