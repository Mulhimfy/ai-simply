Taskade is an all-in-one workspace that blends project management with generative AI. The same document can be viewed as a task list, kanban board, mind map, table, or calendar, and an AI layer sits on top: describe a project in plain language and Taskade drafts the outline, tasks, and subtasks for you. Its more distinctive angle is custom AI agents — you can build assistants tied to a specific workspace or dataset and have them research, draft, or automate recurring steps.

Taskade positions itself as a lighter, faster alternative to heavyweight suites like ClickUp, Asana, and Notion, aimed at small teams and solo operators who want structure without the setup overhead. It runs on web, desktop, and mobile with real-time collaboration, so shared projects update live across everyone's devices.

## Key Features

- Generate full project plans and task breakdowns from a prompt
- Custom AI agents grounded in your workspace content
- Six views of the same data: list, board, mind map, table, calendar, org chart
- Real-time collaboration with chat and video built in
- AI automations for recurring, multi-step workflows
- Large template library spanning many use cases

## Pricing

- **Free**: unlimited projects and collaborators with a monthly AI usage allowance
- **Plus / Pro (~$8–20 per user/month billed annually)**: more AI credits, custom agents, and automation
- **Business / Ultimate (higher tiers)**: larger teams, advanced controls, and analytics

## Best For

Freelancers, solopreneurs, and small remote teams who want tasks, notes, and AI agents in a single fast tool instead of stitching together several apps and switching between them all day.

## Limitations

For large enterprises with complex portfolios, dependencies, and reporting needs, ClickUp, Asana, or Notion still go deeper. Taskade's most useful AI — unlimited credits and custom agents — sits behind its paid plans, and its integration catalog is thinner than the incumbents', so it fits nimble teams better than big, process-heavy organizations.
