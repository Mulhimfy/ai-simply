Clockwise is an AI calendar assistant that reorganizes your schedule to protect deep work. Its core idea is that most people's calendars are pockmarked with scattered 30-minute gaps that are too short to do anything meaningful; Clockwise identifies "flexible" meetings and quietly shifts them to knit those gaps into longer, defensible Focus Time blocks — and it does the same across a team so shared meetings land at times that hurt the fewest people.

Beyond rearranging, it holds Focus Time on your calendar, syncs your Slack status to what you're doing, manages a smart lunch and travel buffers, and surfaces analytics on how your week is actually spent. More recently Clockwise added AI scheduling assistants (including a "Prism" copilot) that can plan and reschedule via natural-language requests.

It is built first and foremost for Google Calendar and Google Workspace teams, with more limited Microsoft Outlook support. Compared with Reclaim.ai — its closest competitor, which is stronger on defending recurring habits and task-based scheduling — Clockwise leans into team-wide meeting optimization and works best when a group adopts it together.

## Key Features

- Automatic Focus Time creation by consolidating flexible meetings
- Team-wide meeting optimization to minimize disruption
- Slack status sync tied to your calendar
- Smart lunch holds and meeting buffers
- Meeting and time-use analytics
- AI scheduling assistant for natural-language planning
- Google Calendar integration (with more limited Outlook support)

## Pricing

- **Free**: Core scheduling and Focus Time for an individual calendar
- **Teams (~$USD 7/user/month)**: Full AI optimization and team coordination features
- **Business (~$USD 12/user/month)**: Advanced analytics and admin controls
- **Enterprise**: Custom, with SSO and larger-org controls

Prices vary with annual billing and periodic plan changes.

## Best For

Knowledge workers and teams — especially on Google Workspace — who want deep work protected automatically and meetings scheduled with less coordination overhead. It pays off most when an entire team is on it.

## Limitations

Clockwise is heavily optimized for Google Calendar, so Outlook-first organizations get a weaker experience. Much of the value depends on team-wide adoption; used solo, the "move everyone's meetings" advantage largely disappears. And automatic rescheduling can feel disruptive at first — it usually needs a bit of tuning before you trust it. If your main need is defending recurring personal habits and tasks, Reclaim.ai is worth comparing.
