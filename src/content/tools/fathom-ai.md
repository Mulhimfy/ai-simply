Fathom is an AI meeting assistant that joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams calls, records and transcribes them, and delivers a structured summary within about 30 seconds of the meeting ending. It was founded in 2020 by Richard White, who previously built the feedback platform UserVoice, and it has become one of the highest-rated products on G2 — often ranked above rivals like Otter.ai, Fireflies, and Gong for user satisfaction.

The unusual part is the pricing. Fathom's free tier includes unlimited recordings and transcription, where competitors like Otter cap free minutes per month. The company monetizes through team plans and CRM-heavy sales use cases instead of squeezing individual users. During a call you can click a highlight button to mark a moment, and Fathom clips it and tags it (action item, objection, question) automatically.

Summaries follow templates you choose — general notes, sales call (BANT, MEDDIC), customer success check-in, one-on-one — and action items get pulled out with owners attached. Everything syncs to HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, Google Docs, or Slack without copy-pasting.

## Key Features

- Unlimited free recording and transcription for Zoom, Meet, and Teams
- AI summary delivered roughly 30 seconds after the call ends
- One-click highlights during the call, auto-clipped and categorized
- Summary templates for sales frameworks like MEDDIC and BANT
- Auto-sync of notes and action items to HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and Notion
- Ask Fathom — chat with a transcript to pull answers from past calls
- Supports transcription in 25+ languages

## Pricing

- **Free**: Unlimited recordings, transcription, and basic AI summaries
- **Premium (~$15/user/month)**: Advanced summary templates, action-item detection, CRM sync
- **Team Edition (~$19/user/month)**: Shared call library, keyword alerts, coaching metrics for managers

## Best For

Salespeople, customer success managers, recruiters, and consultants who live on video calls and want notes flowing into a CRM automatically. It is also a strong pick for anyone who wants a genuinely free notetaker without minute caps.

## Limitations

Fathom joins as a visible bot, so guests know they are being recorded — fine for compliance, awkward with some clients. It is built around scheduled video meetings, so impromptu phone calls and in-person conversations are not its territory; Otter's mobile app handles those better. Team analytics and coaching features sit behind the highest tier.
