Anyword is an AI copywriting platform whose headline feature is prediction, not just generation. Every headline, ad, email, or landing-page block it produces comes with a Predictive Performance Score — a 0–100 estimate of conversion likelihood, trained on data from real marketing campaigns rather than language patterns alone. The idea is simple: generate ten subject lines, then let the score tell you which one to ship.

That focus separates Anyword from general-purpose writers like Jasper and Copy.ai, which optimize for fluency and volume. Anyword is aimed squarely at performance marketers who care about click-through and conversion rates. Its Data-Driven Editor can also score copy you paste in from elsewhere, so it doubles as an audit tool for existing ads and pages.

Rounding it out are Brand Voice training — which learns your tone from existing content — and a library of 200-plus templates covering the usual ad, email, and social formats, plus a blog wizard for longer SEO pieces.

## Key Features

- Predictive Performance Score on every piece of generated copy
- 200+ templates for ads, emails, landing pages, and social posts
- Brand Voice training from your existing content
- Data-Driven Editor that scores and rewrites copy you paste in
- Blog wizard for longer SEO articles
- Team collaboration and approval workflows

## Pricing

- **Starter (~$49/month)**: 1 seat, unlimited words, performance scoring
- **Data-Driven (~$99/month)**: Custom AI models and advanced scoring
- **Business (~$499/month)**: Multiple brand voices and team features
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing
- Free access is limited to a short trial rather than an ongoing free tier

## Best For

Performance marketers, growth teams, and agencies that run paid ads and email at scale — anyone who wants a data-backed reason to pick one headline over another rather than guessing.

## Limitations

The performance scores are directional, not guarantees, and they lean heavily on US, English-language data — treat them as a tie-breaker, not gospel, in other markets. There's no ongoing free plan, and pricing runs higher than general writers like Copy.ai. If you mostly need long-form articles rather than conversion copy, a dedicated SEO tool such as Frase or Surfer will serve you better.
