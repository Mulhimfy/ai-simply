Alphascope is an AI research workspace for prediction-market traders. Instead of bouncing between Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold, and Opinion tabs, it pulls live odds from all four platforms into one dashboard and layers analysis on top: AI probability forecasts for market outcomes, real-time probability charts with trend analysis, and a news feed that ranks breaking stories by how much they're likely to move specific markets.

Because it watches the same question priced on multiple platforms at once, Alphascope can flag cross-platform discrepancies — the same event trading at different implied probabilities on Polymarket and Kalshi — along with mispriced markets and unusual volume via smart alerts. Filters by platform, category, probability range, and volume help narrow thousands of open markets down to the ones worth researching.

## Key Features

- Live odds and market data aggregated from Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold, and Opinion
- AI probability forecasting for market outcomes
- News-impact analysis that ranks stories by relevance to open markets
- Real-time probability charts with trend analysis
- Cross-platform arbitrage detection for pricing discrepancies between venues
- Smart alerts for mispriced markets and unusual volume
- Filtering by platform, category, probability range, and volume

## Pricing

Free to get started with no credit card required; the core dashboard, forecasts, and alerts are usable on the free tier. Portfolio tracking, automated strategies, and AI-generated market reports are listed as upcoming premium features.

## Best For

Active prediction-market traders who follow multiple venues and want one place to spot mispriced markets, compare odds across platforms, and connect breaking news to the markets it will actually move.
