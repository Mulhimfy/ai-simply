Duolingo is the world's most downloaded education app, teaching 40+ languages through short, gamified lessons. Its formula — bite-sized exercises, daily streaks, XP, and competitive leagues — turns language study into a habit, which is a large part of why it has hundreds of millions of registered learners. The company, founded by Luis von Ahn (co-creator of reCAPTCHA), is publicly traded and known for its aggressive, meme-friendly marketing.

AI runs throughout the product. Lesson difficulty adapts to your mistakes, and the premium Duolingo Max tier adds generative-AI features powered by GPT-4: Roleplay lets you hold a text conversation with AI characters in scenarios like ordering coffee, and Explain My Answer breaks down why a response was wrong. Max competes less with classroom apps and more with AI tutors like ChatGPT's voice mode.

## Key Features

- 40+ languages including Japanese, Mandarin, Arabic, and Welsh
- Adaptive lessons that adjust to your error patterns
- Roleplay — AI conversation practice with characters (Max)
- Explain My Answer — in-depth grammar feedback (Max)
- Listening, speaking, and pronunciation exercises
- Streaks, XP, leaderboards, and leagues for motivation
- Duolingo for Schools dashboard for classroom teachers

## Pricing

- **Free**: Full course access with ads and limited "hearts"
- **Super Duolingo (around $7/month billed annually)**: No ads, unlimited hearts, extra practice
- **Duolingo Max (around $14/month or more)**: Adds Roleplay and Explain My Answer

## Best For

Beginners and intermediate learners who want a low-pressure daily habit for building vocabulary and grammar foundations, and teachers who want a free classroom tool. Great as a supplement to travel, coursework, or immersion.

## Limitations

Duolingo builds vocabulary and recognition well but rarely produces conversational fluency on its own — most learners still need real speaking practice or a tutor. Duolingo Max's AI features cost noticeably more and are limited to certain language pairs. The gamification, while motivating, can nudge users toward protecting a streak rather than genuinely progressing.
