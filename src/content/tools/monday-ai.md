monday.com is a flexible Work OS — a visual platform of customizable boards that teams use for project management, CRM, and operations. Its AI features (marketed as monday AI, including "monday Expert" and AI Blocks) sit inside those boards rather than in a separate app, so the assistant works directly with the tasks, columns, and updates you already have.

The practical uses are workflow-focused: generate a project plan or task breakdown from a short description, summarize a busy board or long update thread, draft emails and replies, and — usefully — build automations and formulas by describing them in plain English instead of learning the syntax. It can also categorize and extract data from text columns. Compared with Asana Intelligence or ClickUp Brain, monday's edge is how deeply the AI ties into its highly visual, configurable board model.

## Key Features

- Generate project plans and task lists from a description
- AI board and update summaries for quick status reporting
- Natural-language automation and formula builder
- Draft emails, replies, and item updates
- Text classification and data extraction in columns
- AI Blocks that add AI steps into custom workflows

## Pricing

- **Free**: Up to 2 seats and core boards (no AI)
- **Basic**: ~$12/seat/month, unlimited items
- **Standard**: ~$14/seat/month, where AI features become available
- **Pro**: ~$24/seat/month, advanced automations and analytics
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing with SSO and advanced security

## Best For

Project managers, operations teams, and agencies that want a flexible, visual work platform where AI handles the busywork of status summaries, plan drafting, and no-code automation setup.

## Limitations

AI is gated to the Standard plan and above, and monday meters AI actions with credits that can be exhausted on heavy use. The platform's flexibility is a double-edged sword — new teams often face a setup and configuration curve before the AI has well-structured boards to work with. Teams wanting deeper documents or wikis may still pair it with another tool.
