Klaviyo is an email and SMS marketing platform built specifically for e-commerce, and it has become the default choice for serious direct-to-consumer brands on Shopify. What sets it apart from general-purpose senders like Mailchimp or Constant Contact is how tightly it binds to store data: every customer profile carries full purchase history, browsing behavior, and predicted metrics, so segments and flows react to what people actually buy rather than just who opened an email.

Its AI layer covers predictive customer lifetime value, churn-risk scoring, expected date of next order, and AI-suggested subject lines and send times. Klaviyo also folds SMS, MMS, and mobile push into the same contact list, so a single abandoned-cart flow can move a shopper across channels without duplicate tooling. The company went public (NYSE: KVYO) in 2023 and partners closely with Shopify.

## Key Features

- Predictive analytics: customer lifetime value, churn risk, and predicted next-order date
- Pre-built e-commerce flows (abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back)
- Revenue attribution reporting per flow, campaign, and segment
- AI subject-line and send-time optimization
- Deep native integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce
- Unified email, SMS/MMS, and mobile push on one profile
- Behavior-based segmentation with unlimited custom properties

## Pricing

- **Free**: Up to 250 contacts, 500 email sends/month, 150 SMS credits
- **Email**: From roughly $20/month, scaling by active contact count
- **Email + SMS**: Combined plan priced by contacts plus SMS usage

## Best For

Growing Shopify and WooCommerce brands that want segmentation and automation driven by real purchase data, plus clear revenue attribution. Klaviyo shines once you have enough order history for its predictive models to matter.

## Limitations

Pricing climbs steeply with list size, so large but low-engagement lists get costly — pruning inactive contacts is almost mandatory. It is also heavier than most beginners need for a plain newsletter, and teams outside e-commerce may find simpler, cheaper tools like Mailchimp or Beehiiv a better fit.
