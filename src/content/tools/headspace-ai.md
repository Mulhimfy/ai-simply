Headspace is one of the most established meditation and mindfulness apps, known for its friendly, animation-driven design and structured courses that walk beginners through the basics of meditation. Its catalog spans guided meditations, Sleepcasts and sleep sounds, focus soundscapes, short breathing exercises, and movement content, organized around goals like stress, anxiety, sleep, and focus.

The AI layer is Ebb, an empathetic AI companion introduced to make the app more personalized. Ebb can hold a reflective, conversational check-in, help you name what you're feeling, and then point you to a relevant meditation or exercise — closer to a guided journaling companion than a chatbot. Headspace is careful to frame Ebb as a wellness tool, not a replacement for therapy, and the company also runs Headspace Health for clinical and employer-provided care.

Its closest consumer rival is Calm, which leans more on sleep stories and celebrity narration; Headspace differentiates with its teaching-oriented, course-based structure and now with Ebb's personalization.

## Key Features

- Hundreds of guided meditations organized by goal and skill level
- Ebb AI companion for reflective check-ins and session recommendations
- Sleepcasts, sleep sounds, and wind-down content
- Focus music and soundscapes for work
- Short breathing and SOS exercises for acute stress
- Progress tracking, streaks, and structured beginner courses

## Pricing

- **Free**: a limited selection plus a trial of the full library
- **Plus (~$13/month or ~$70/year)**: full library, Ebb, and all features
- **Family (~$20/month)**: covers multiple members
- **Student/Employer**: discounted student plans and Headspace-for-work coverage

## Best For

Meditation beginners, and busy professionals managing stress, burnout, or sleep, who want a polished, guided, and now AI-personalized on-ramp to a mindfulness habit.

## Limitations

Most of the value is behind the subscription, so the free experience is thin beyond the trial. Ebb is a supportive wellness companion, not clinical care — it is not a substitute for a therapist or crisis line. Experienced meditators who prefer unguided or timer-based practice may also find the heavily curated, course-driven format restrictive.
