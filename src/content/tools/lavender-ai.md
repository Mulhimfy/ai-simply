Lavender takes the opposite approach to most AI sales tools. Instead of generating hundreds of templated emails, it acts as a writing coach: as a rep types, Lavender scores the email from 0 to 100 and flags what statistically hurts reply rates — long paragraphs, too many "I" statements, weak subject lines, walls of text on mobile. The recommendations come from analysis of millions of real sales emails, not generic writing rules.

That coaching angle is what separates it from Instantly or Smartlead, which optimize for volume. Lavender optimizes the individual email, which makes it a natural companion to those platforms rather than a replacement. It sits inside the tools reps already use — Gmail, Outlook, Outreach, Salesloft, and HubSpot — as a sidebar in the compose window.

The second half of the product is the personalization assistant. Before writing, Lavender surfaces the prospect's LinkedIn activity, company news, funding events, and tech stack next to the email, so research that once took ten minutes of tab-hopping happens in one place. Managers get team dashboards showing whose emails score well and which coaching points come up repeatedly.

## Key Features

- Real-time email scoring (0–100) with specific fix suggestions
- Personalization panel with prospect data, company news, and personality insights
- Subject line and opener recommendations tied to reply-rate data
- Mobile preview showing how the email renders on a phone
- Integrations with Gmail, Outlook, Outreach, Salesloft, and HubSpot
- Team analytics and coaching dashboards for sales managers
- Free "Lavender for Good" access for students and job seekers

## Pricing

- **Free**: limited number of email analyses per month with basic scoring
- **Individual Pro**: roughly $29/month for unlimited emails and full personalization features
- **Teams**: around $49/user/month adding manager dashboards, coaching analytics, and admin controls
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing with security review and dedicated support

## Best For

SDRs and account executives who send targeted, researched outreach rather than mass blasts; sales managers who want data on why some reps get replies and others don't; and job seekers writing cold applications, who get it free.

## Limitations

Lavender improves emails one at a time, so it won't help if your strategy is 1,000 sends a day — pair it with a sequencer instead. The per-seat cost is hard to justify for teams that mostly send templated sequences, and some users find the Gmail extension adds noticeable load time.
