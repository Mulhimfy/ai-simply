Adobe Express (formerly Adobe Spark) is Adobe's answer to Canva — a browser and mobile app for quickly making social posts, flyers, logos, thumbnails and short videos without opening Photoshop. It leans on Adobe's Firefly generative AI for text-to-image, generative fill, text effects and background removal, and its templates and stock feel more polished than most casual tools.

The key differentiator from Canva is Firefly. Adobe trained Firefly on Adobe Stock and licensed/public-domain content and offers indemnification on some plans, which makes generated images lower-risk for commercial work than models with murkier training data. Express also plugs into the wider Creative Cloud world, so assets and fonts can move between it and Photoshop or Illustrator. For anyone already paying for Creative Cloud, Express is bundled in.

## Key Features

- Firefly text-to-image and generative fill inside the editor
- Tens of thousands of templates for social, print, and video
- One-click background removal and object cleanup
- Text effects, animation, and simple video editing with music
- Brand kit for consistent logos, colors, and fonts
- Instant resize to reformat one design for every platform
- Adobe Stock, Adobe Fonts, and Creative Cloud asset access

## Pricing

- **Free**: Core editor, many templates, Adobe Fonts, limited generative credits
- **Premium (around $9.99/month)**: Premium templates and assets, more Firefly generative credits, brand kit
- **Included**: Bundled with most Creative Cloud subscriptions

## Best For

Small-business owners, social media managers, students, and marketers who want on-brand graphics and short videos fast — especially those already in the Adobe ecosystem who want commercially safer AI images.

## Limitations

It is a simplification tool, not a professional editor: you trade Photoshop and Illustrator's precision for speed and ease. Paid plans meter Firefly generative credits, so heavy AI users can hit monthly caps. Canva remains ahead on sheer template volume and team collaboration features, so the choice often comes down to whether you value Firefly's licensing story or Canva's ecosystem.
